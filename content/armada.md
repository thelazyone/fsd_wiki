The Armada is the last of the great Empires’ fleet that survived the [Schism](@/history.md#the-schism) relatively unscathed. Unbound from the [struggles?] of the New Nations that formed in the decades that followed, they became the largest space fleet of the System.

Nearly a century later, they still play a central role in anything that happens outside of the main planets’ surroundings, and often helped the [RPA](@/reclaiming-and-prospecting-authority.md) in maintaining balance between the minor [stakeholders](@/the-stakeholders.md).

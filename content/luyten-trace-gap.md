[Luyten](@/luyten.md) had been evaluated for colonization in the early days of the [Human Diaspora](@/human-diaspora.md), but it soon revealed a puzzling and unique characteristic: the spectrum analysis of any of its planets and planetoids showed an anomalously low amount of Phosphorus, and a complete absence of three other elements: Arsenic, Selenium and Bismuth. Scientists were left wondering on the reason of the absence, and found none. Following the discovery, the same trace gap was measured in other star systems with similar spectral type and luminosity class.

When, during the second wave of colonization attempts, a convoy was sent to Luyten, it carried an enormous stockpile of the missing elements. The flora engineered for the planets terraforming effort was engineered to require the least amount of the Trace Gap elements - but no life form can truly live without them.

The economy of the System in the centuries that followed developed steadily, but always relied on further shipments of the “Missing Quartet”. Due to the amount of interstellar ships that kept crossing Luyten’s Inner System, people started referring to the four elements (P, As, Se, Bi) as the “Passby” gap.

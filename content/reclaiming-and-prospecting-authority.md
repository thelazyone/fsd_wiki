In order to regulate the access to the [Themis Exclusion Zone](@/themis-exclusion-zone.md), the Reclaiming and Prospecting Authority was formed, as an independent organization with the role of arbiter between the various interested [stakeholders](@/the-stakeholders.md).

In order to defend its sovereignty, most of the initial capital was invested in forming a large fleet to protect the Exclusion Zone from any interference, and that alone brought a much needed influx of jobs and money to the off-planet human settlements, that suffered more than anything else during the [Schism](@/history.md#the-schism), well before the arrival of any of the debris of the [Cornucopia](@/cornucopia.md).

By the time the first cargo crates started lighting bright streaks of plasma over the [Themis](@/themis.md) atmosphere, the RPA had become already a well-established entity in the System.

Favoring currency over ideology, the RPA had the time to mediate and carve out the Themis region between different parties, to ensure that everyone could make an enormous profit without risking a new System-wide war.

Meanwhile, to maximize the recovery effort, it also started minting [Salvage Charters](@/salvage-charters.md) for minor organizations interested in fighting over the leftovers, too scattered or not valuable enough to be worth the attention of the larger players in the System.

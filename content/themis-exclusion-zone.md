The distress signals and the detailed logs of the critical failure managed to reach every major faction of the System, but not many could do something about it while fighting a bloody and widespread war against the [Tech forces](@/the-tech.md).

Once the war reduced in intensity, many of the remaining nations and powers started minor scuffles trying to secure the most likely areas for the precious debris to arrive in. Soon, however, it became clear that cooperation was in the interest of everyone, and that the best way to secure as much of the precious cargo as possible was to designate the region around [themis](@/themis.md), including the two Lagrange zones on its sides, as an exclusion zone where no commercial activity was allowed without a specific [Charter](@/salvage-charters.md).

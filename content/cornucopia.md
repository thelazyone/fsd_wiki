The Hierarchy-Class Interstellar Hauler Cornucopia left the [Procyon binary system](@/procyon.md) fully loaded: the cargo registers mention 200 MTons of a blend of the  [Missing Quartet](@/luyten-trace-gap.md), 80 containers of sentient cores, and similar amounts of high grade processors, nanites and rare isotopes. It also carried a shipment of animal embryos and several new strands of engineered plants for research and terraforming experiments.

The estimated time of travel was 38 months. The departure happened two years and a half before the [Schism](@/history.md#the-schism), 93 years before the present day.

With the [Beacons](@/beacon.md) destroyed, the engines of the Cornucopia could only perform part of the deceleration and, well before reaching the orbit of the outer planets, its inertial engines safeguard activated, causing a critical deceleration and tearing itself apart.

With its speed drastically reduced, the scattered pieces of the Cornucopia and its cargo continued drifting across the orbit of [Ruun](@/ruun.md), and soon the gravitational pull of [Themis](@/themis.md) started affecting its trajectory, capturing them in a myriad of orbits, many sinking through the surface of the gas giant.

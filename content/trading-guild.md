The Trading Guild has the most to gain from any resource extracted from the [Cornucopia](@/cornucopia.md): regardless of who controls the cargo, its trade routes, freight contracts, and storage networks, money will eventually come
trickling down into the Guild’s coffers.

Their main interest is to maintain an effective supply chain for everyone to keep extracting and bringing new resources to the market. Any disruption of this is seen by them as a profit loss, and therefore a threat.

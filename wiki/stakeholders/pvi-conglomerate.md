# Pan-Virgo Industries

The Pan-Virgo Industries, or PVI, are one of the three prominent corporations controlling the Luyten system economy.
They are part of the New Trust, also known as the Conglomerate, which ratified a truce between the corporations after the [Schism](history#the-schism).

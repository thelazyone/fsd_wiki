# Luyten
Amongst those Systems that suffered such complete isolation in the [Schism](history#the-schism), Luyten was typical of the mid-era colonisation expansion. After the Singularity, the [Hive AI](hive-ai) did not wipe out the population but dealt major losses to the humans, and though initially driven from the system by the humans’ resistance, the terrifying prospect of their legacy lives on. 

Hushed rumours talk of caches of dormant [Tech forces](the-tech), hidden in vaults and old, forgotten structures across the moons and wild, undiscovered areas of Luyten’s planets.

Humanity’s basic drives and motivations had not changed though. Despite millennia of technolological advancement and sociological progression, the need for power and control over resources and people were once again revealed as people suffered the hardships of their lives after the Schism. 

Many different factions rose up to gain control of the system’s resources: none have yet prevailed. Despite some Nations and Corporations laying claim to a moon or a continent, most of the System is still contended; conflict abounds.

## The Inner Space

[Sauguro](sauguro), the innermost planet, is mostly a barren rock which was terraformed in the years immediately preceding the Singularity and, despite the harshness of the conditions on the surface, still holds a breathable atmosphere. Extremely rich in resources, especially those needed for the substrates used in computational matrices, it is a precious target for many who covet its hidden riches.

[Claeo](claeo), or New Earth, is one of the rare planets with a natural atmosphere and liquid water. On its surface there are a plethora of biomes, and some indigenous flora and fauna. This jewel in the Luyten system is coveted by many: rich, fertile and eminently habitable, many vie for ownership of swathes of its most attractive continents. 
The two moons of Claeo act as spaceports and host a handful of relatively small but teeming settlements.

[Archon](archon) is a rocky planet with a higher gravity that was an early subject of the colonial terraforming programme, and is now covered in cunningly geo-engineered pseudo-vegetation, that provides atmosphere and temperature control within tolerable limits at the planetary surface. Archon is subject to highly active geological mantle activity, and the resulting violent plate tectonics have created sharp and convoluted features throughout the its surface.
The only moon of a considerable size is constantly hit by dust and small asteroids coming from the planet’s two rings and is almost entirely uninhabited.

## The Outer Space
[Themis](themis) is a gas giant, around which there are three inhabited moons: Domu, Maat and Norn. The planet also marks the beginning of the outer space of the System, with a much lower density of asteroids and comets. 

[Domu](domu) and [Norn](norn) are rocky moons, both with a nearly breathable athmosphere. Domu population is larger, and have been genetically engineered to be able to withstand the high level of hydrocarbons in the air. Norn have been hit harder during the Schism, and most of its factory-cities are now not operational, with only small groups living in them.
[Maat](maat) is a much colder moon, almost entirely covered in a sea of liquid methane. There are still two operational deuterium harvesters floating on the surface, surrounded by a small community of service workers.

The [Giano Habitat](giano-habitat), the only fully functioning habitat of the System, follows Themis in its L1 Lagrangian orbit, away from any disturbance that the moons could provide.

[Ruun](ruun), the outermost planet of the Luyten system, is a cold and desolate planet with an unusual level of active geothermal activity. This geothermal activity results in vast dust storms, shot through with extreme electrical discharges and deafening thunder. An awe-inspiring sight to be sure, though this extreme weather makes human habitation extremely difficult on the surface.

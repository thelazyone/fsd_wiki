## The Factions

There are currently six factions in the FSD universe. Each has a unique design and gameplay style, with a full range of different units, characters, and support cards. 
Each faction can be used to represent several different in-lore groups, nations or armies, all sharing the same Unit profiles but with a different background in the setting.
